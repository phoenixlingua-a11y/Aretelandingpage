02 · The rule that makes it usable

Signals. Never their answers.

What you see

The reading, the drift, the direction, the named blindspots, and a prepared line of enquiry for the session.

What you never see

What they wrote. What they answered. What they told the instrument at six in the morning. That boundary is enforced in code, not promised in a policy.

Clients join by explicit, named consent, and they can leave with one tap and take their record with them. Data is EU-resident, Frankfurt. Zero trackers, zero third parties.

This matters because the alternative breaks the relationship. A client who suspects you are reading their diary stops telling the instrument the truth, and then it measures nothing.
